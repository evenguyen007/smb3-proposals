Sample 01 / Grief

The Wet Dog Theory of Grief

Hook
Nobody explains grief like this, but I think everyone needs to hear it.
Script
Nobody warns you that grief doesn't follow you around crying. Sometimes it just sits quietly in the corner. And you think you're fine. You're functioning. You're present. And then it stands up, shakes itself off, and soaks you. In the middle of a work meeting. On a random Tuesday. When someone says a word that sounds like their name. That is not a sign that something is wrong. That is not falling apart. That is grief being exactly what grief is. It doesn't ask permission. It doesn't read the room. It just shakes. And the kindest thing you can do is let it.
Sample 02 / Mental Health

The Thought vs. The Unmet Need (Part 2)

Hook
There's a reason some things keep coming back no matter how many times you let them go.
Script
A thought? You can acknowledge it, thank it for its contribution, and watch it float right on by. Classic mindfulness. It works. An unmet need? An unmet need is not floating anywhere. It will text you at 11pm. It will sit next to you at family dinner. It will show up in your body as tension, withdrawal, or that one thing you keep doing that you wish you would stop doing. Because an unmet need is not a thought. It is information. And information doesn't disappear when you ignore it. It just gets louder until you listen.
Sample 03 / Grief

Grief Doesn't Require a Death

Hook
A non-clinical check-in for anyone who might be grieving something they haven't named yet.
Script
Grief doesn't require a death. You can grieve a version of yourself you had to let go of. A relationship that ended differently than you planned. A future you had fully mapped out. A parent who is still alive but hasn't been present in years. If you've been feeling something you can't quite name. If something feels off and you keep waiting for it to pass. If you're fine, but also somehow not. It might be grief. And it counts. Whatever it is. It counts.
