Sample work

Sample 01 / Grief Reel
The Group Chat
Caption
Been sitting with this analogy for a while. Grief is the onionest of onions but the group chat thing... yeah.
Script
When someone you love dies, your brain doesn't leave the group chat. You still want to tag them in things. You still save articles to share later. You still catch yourself calculating whether it's their time zone. That is not something going wrong. That is a brain that loved someone. The relationship doesn't end when the person does. It just goes quiet. And sometimes the hardest part of grief is sitting with all the notifications you never got to send.
Sample 02 / Mental Health Reel
The Low Battery
Caption
Me and the kitschy analogy format, we're committed to the bit forever and always.
Script
You know when your phone hits 20% and starts doing those things? Dims the screen. Kills the background apps. Refuses to do anything that isn't essential. That's not your phone being dramatic. That's your phone conserving what it has left. Your nervous system does the same thing. When you're exhausted and can't handle the thing you normally handle, when you're short with people you love, when you just... can't, That's not a character flaw. That is a low battery warning. Biology doesn't ask permission. It just starts dimming the screen.
Sample 03 / Mental Health Reel
The Forwarded Message
Caption
Incredibly not actually sorry to report that your anxiety might not even be yours.
Script
Some of the worry you carry was forwarded to you. From a parent who taught you the world wasn't safe. From a childhood that asked you to be ready for something bad. From a body that learned: anticipate. That is not weakness. That is adaptive. Your nervous system did exactly what it was supposed to do. It took notes. The work isn't unlearning the worry. The work is figuring out which messages actually belong to you.
