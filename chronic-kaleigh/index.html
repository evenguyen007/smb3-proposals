Sample 1 · Carousel script

Newly Diagnosed with POTS — Save This

Slide 1 · Cover
Newly diagnosed with POTS? Save this for your next appointment. 🩷
Visual: Soft pink background, your handwritten title overlay, ✨ chronic.kaleigh small in the corner.
Slide 2 · Re-frame
POTS is NOT a heart condition.
It's a type of Dysautonomia — an autonomic nervous system disorder. Your heart rate spikes when you stand because your nervous system is misfiring, not because your heart is broken.
Slide 3 · Diagnostic criteria
The criteria (NIH 2019 Expert Consensus)
Adults: HR rise of ≥30 bpm within 10 minutes of standing. Teens: ≥40 bpm. Sustained, with no significant drop in blood pressure. Source: PMC8455420
Slide 4 · What to ask for
Ask your doctor for these — minimum.
✨ Echocardiogram  ✨ EKG  ✨ Holter monitor  ✨ Active stand test or formal tilt table  ✨ Orthostatic vitals at 3, 5, and 10 minutes.
Slide 5 · The trap
‼️ Don't let them sit you down between readings.
Sitting between supine and standing readings can mask your true autonomic response. The diagnostic criteria is supine → standing. Not sitting in between.
Slide 6 · Symptoms
POTS isn't just heart-related.
Tachycardia · lightheadedness · GI issues · exercise intolerance · fatigue · shortness of breath · brain fog · chest pain. Symptoms span multiple body systems because the autonomic nervous system runs them.
Slide 7 · What you can do today
While you wait for a specialist:
✨ Increase fluids  ✨ Salt as tolerated  ✨ Compression garments  ✨ Pacing  ✨ Track symptoms with timestamps — your data matters when "rest more" doesn't cut it.
Slide 8 · Send-off
You're not crazy. You're not lazy. You're not making it up.
Send this to a friend who needs it. 🩷
Sources: PMC8455420 · Dysautonomia International · NIH 2019 Expert Consensus Meeting
