What I noticed

Most people who get a 1.15 million view reel pivot. They drop the rest of their work and become a "nervous system creator" or a "Bali creator" or a "self-publishing creator." You won't do that. I think that's the right call.

I read 100 of your posts before I wrote a word of this. The line that decided this proposal was this one:

The conventional advice would be to niche down. To pick "doing nothing" or to pick "Bali" or to pick "self-publishing" and force the algorithm to rank you for it. The conventional advice would also miss why you got the viral in the first place. The "doing nothing" reel was not a wellness post. It was a self-publisher in Bali, in her own home, sitting still next to a partner she met at the gym, with a six-month book project hanging over her head, doing nothing on purpose. The mosaic is the content.

What needs to happen is not a niche. It is a frame strong enough to hold all the pieces. You already wrote the frame. "Open-world game." "Stone Age brain." "Mosaic." The strategy is to stop letting those metaphors live inside one caption at a time and let them carry the whole grid.

02

Three things in the data

After 100 posts and the back end of your numbers, three patterns stood out. None of them require you to change voice. All three are reversible inside 30 days.

Observation 01 · Cadence

Your output peaked at 23 posts in January, the month of the viral, then halved. The momentum is leaking now.

October to January, 65 posts. February to April, 35 posts. The 1.15M view reel is from December. Most accounts that hit one viral see follower growth for 60 to 90 days afterward, then slow attrition unless they ship a follow-up category. You are inside that window right now.

Source · 100-post audit, monthly cadence
Observation 02 · Format

91% Reels, 7% carousels. Your highest comment rate is on carousels.

Reels averaged 27 comments. Carousels averaged 60. You have almost zero saved, screenshotted, or shared content despite a viral worth 99,720 likes. A carousel version of the same content saves and shares in ways a reel structurally cannot.

Source · format breakdown, n=100
Observation 03 · Lead capture

The viral reel generated 99,720 likes and zero captured leads. The pattern that would have captured them already lives in your account.

Comment "Kintamani" got 84 comments. Comment "bali view" got 68. Comment "jungle sauna" got 53. You already use comment-trigger CTAs for Bali content and they work. The same pattern was missing from the doing-nothing series. Same audience, same instinct, no funnel.

Source · top comment-trigger posts, Kintamani, bali view
