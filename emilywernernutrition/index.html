02

Three things in the data

After 100 posts and the engagement numbers behind them, three patterns stood out. None of them require you to change your voice. All three are fixable without adding a single new content idea.

Observation 01 · Format

Your carousels generate 7× more comments than your reels, but you publish one carousel for every eight reels.

Reels average 2.5 comments per post. Carousels average 17.5. Your format mix is 88% Reels and 11% Carousels. The pregnancy announcement carousel (170 comments, 506 likes) is your highest-performing post by every metric. Carousels save and share in ways reels structurally cannot — they are the format that builds word-of-mouth at the sub-5K account stage.

Source: 100-post format audit. Carousel n=11 vs Reel n=88.

Observation 02 · Story vs. Educational

Your personal story content generates 5–10× the engagement of your educational reels, but it's only about 10% of what you post.

Your three highest-comment posts are all story content: the pregnancy announcement (170C), the 19-weeks post (53C), and the 26-weeks update (33C). Your best educational reel sits at 36 comments. Most educational posts get zero. The asymmetry is the opportunity — you are putting 90% of your output into the format that earns the least engagement, and holding back the one that earns the most.

Source: top-comment post breakdown, story vs. educational segmentation.

Observation 03 · Hooks

Your 84,550-view reel proves the algorithm will distribute you. Your median of 289 views proves most reels are not earning the first 1.5 seconds.

The viral reel ("It's less about restriction and more about understanding which foods tend to pour fuel on the inflammation") has a listicle-promise hook with permission framing. The post-surgery reel ("You're not crazy for wondering if it actually worked") names a specific feeling. The majority of your educational reels open on a generic statement or mid-explanation. The hook gap between 84k views and 289 views is not algorithm luck — it is the difference between earning the first second and not.

Source: post DXW_D_NkQnE (84,550 views) vs. median across 88 reels.
