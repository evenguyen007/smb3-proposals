01 / What I see

Four things I noticed reading your last 100 posts.

I went through every caption, every Reel, every comment thread. Here is what the data actually says.

1
Your viral formula has a name — and you are already using it.

Every one of your top posts follows the same structure: take a therapy fear or misconception, invert it, make the viewer feel understood. "Therapy is not fixing you" (32K views). "Why therapists ask about childhood" (34K). "When you had to grow up too fast" (67K). "Problems are subjective" (144K). All variations on the same reframe. Most accounts never figure this out. You already have.

2
The parentification lane is your highest-performing niche and you have barely touched it.

"Grow up too fast" hit 67,000 views. The "mature for your age" carousel generated saves and meaningful comments. This audience — adults who managed the room before they knew how to ask for help — is everywhere in your content themes but gets maybe 15% of your posts. It deserves 30%.

3
You have zero save-worthy content.

Your audience are high-achievers. They screenshot resources. They save carousels to revisit. Right now you have almost no carousel content — 12 in 100 posts, none structured as a save-and-reference educational piece. That is a direct cap on algorithm depth-of-engagement signals and a lost retention tool for exactly the kind of follower who books consults.

4
97 of your last 100 posts have zero hashtags.

For a geography-dependent practice — virtual across Ontario, in-person Burlington — hashtag discovery is the lowest-effort reach lever you are not using. A targeted stack of 10 to 15 tags per post (#therapyontario, #highfunctioninganxiety, #peoplepleaserhealing, #burlingtontherapist) could meaningfully expand reach without changing a single thing about your content.

03 / Sample work

Two pieces, written in your voice.

Sample A / Carousel (6 slides)

"5 Signs You Have High-Functioning Anxiety (And Why They're So Easy to Dismiss)"

6-slide educational carousel · High-functioning anxiety niche · CTA: book free consult

Slide 01 / Cover

"You seem fine. But inside, it never stops."

5 signs of high-functioning anxiety, even when life looks together.

Slide 02

"Your to-do list is how you feel safe."

You do not plan because you love organizing. You plan because your nervous system needs somewhere to put the worry. The moment your calendar clears, the anxiety floods in.

Slide 03

"You apologize before you know what you did wrong."

Automatic. Before you have had a second to assess, sorry is already out of your mouth. That reflex is not politeness. It is what happens when being "too much" was once a real threat.

Slide 04

"Rest makes you more anxious than being busy."

A free day should feel like relief. Instead it feels restless, guilty, on edge. Because being busy was never about productivity. It was about staying one step ahead of the feelings.

Slide 05

"You have been called 'mature for your age' your whole life."

What that actually meant: you learned to manage the room before you were old enough to need to. Those early patterns do not disappear. They show up as anxiety in adulthood.

Slide 06 / CTA

"You are not broken. You adapted."

High-functioning anxiety is real, exhausting, and responds to therapy. If 2 or more of these landed, this is worth exploring. Book a free consult at Everpath.
