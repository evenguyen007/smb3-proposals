02  /  3 things I noticed

What I saw when I audited your account.

01
Your carousels are outperforming your Reels nearly 2 to 1, but they're only 11% of your content.

Across 100 posts analyzed, your carousels averaged 10.3 comments each. Your Reels averaged 4.7. Your IFS and inner child carousel alone received 21 comments, outperforming almost every Reel in the same period. But right now you're posting Reels 79% of the time.

The data

Carousel avg: 10.3 comments  ·  Reel avg: 4.7 comments. Your highest-engagement format is your least-used one.

The opportunity

Moving to 2 to 3 carousels per week could meaningfully increase engagement without changing how you think about content or adding more to your plate.

02
You have no lead magnet. Warm followers have nowhere to go.

Every CTA on your account points to "link in bio to book a consultation." That's a big ask for someone who just discovered you. They read your content, feel something, want to stay connected, but there's no next step short of booking therapy. People who aren't ready yet quietly disappear.

The data

100% of your CTAs lead directly to a consultation booking. No email opt-in, no freebie, no soft entry point.

The opportunity

A simple free guide, something like "5 signs you're people-pleasing in your relationships," paired with an email opt-in, would keep warm traffic in your world long after the post is gone.

03
"Currently accepting new clients" in almost every post is quietly working against your authority.

The intention is good, but the effect is the opposite of what you want: it signals that you're waiting for clients, not that clients are trying to find you. Your two most viral Reels, 101K and 79K views, had no pitch at all. Personal content with no selling pulled the most people.

The data

Your 101K and 79K view Reels both had short, personal captions with no availability CTA. Your typical Reels with the phrase average 200 to 1,000 views.

The opportunity

Replace it with "A few spots are now open" and use it once per week. Let the rest of your content do the authority-building work it's actually good at.
