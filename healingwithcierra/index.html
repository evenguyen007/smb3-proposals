02  /  3 dieu em quan sat

Nhung dieu em nhin thay trong account cua chi.

01
Carousel cua chi dang hoat dong tot hon Reels gan 2 lan, nhung chi chiem 11% noi dung.

Bang chung

Co hoi

Tang len 2 den 3 carousels moi tuan co the lam tang engagement dang ke ma khong can them Reels hay thay doi cach chi suy nghi ve content.

02
Chi khong co lead magnet. Follower warm dang mat chua kip chao.

100% CTA cua chi deu la "link in bio to book a consultation." Day la mot buoc qua lon doi voi nguoi chi moi biet den chi. Ho doc content, cam nhan, muon tim hieu them, nhung khong co buoc tiep theo nao ngoai viec tu nhien book mot buoi tri lieu.

Bang chung

Khong co email opt-in, khong co freebie, khong co lead capture. Nguoi follow chi ma chua san sang book co the ro di bat cu luc nao.

Co hoi

Mot free guide don gian, vi du "5 signs you're people-pleasing in your relationships," ket hop email opt-in se giu warm traffic o lai va nuoi duong ho thanh khach hang theo thoi gian.

03
Framing "currently accepting new clients" dang vo tinh lam yeu di authority cua chi.

Cau nay xuat hien trong gan moi bai dang. Y dinh tot, nhung hieu ung nguoc lai: nghe co ve chi dang cho khach hang thay vi khach hang dang tim chi. Hai bai Reels viral nhat cua chi (101K va 79K views) khong co pitch nao. Content ca nhan, khong selling, lai keo nhieu nguoi nhat.

Bang chung

101K views va 79K views la hai bai Reels khong co "currently accepting clients." Cac bai co cau nay thuong chi dat 200 den 1,000 views.

Co hoi

Thay bang "A few spots are now open" va chi dung 1 lan moi tuan. De phan noi dung con lai xay dung authority thay vi pitch.

04  /  Sample em da viet thu cho chi

Sample 1: Carousel

7 slides  ·  People-pleasing + Nervous system

Slide 1 / Cover

5 signs your nervous system learned to stay quiet (instead of saying no)

Slide 2

1. You feel sudden exhaustion the moment someone makes a request.

Not because you're overwhelmed by the task. Because your body may have already said yes before you had a chance to think it through.

Slide 3

2. You over-explain every limit you try to set.

As if the boundary needs a defense. As if your needs aren't reason enough on their own.

Slide 4

3. You feel guilty for days after setting a limit, even when it was completely reasonable.

That guilt often isn't weakness. It's what staying safe used to cost you.

Slide 5

4. Your stomach tightens right before you say something honest.

That's your nervous system anticipating a response it learned to fear. It's still trying to protect you.

Slide 6

5. You feel a wave of relief when someone cancels, and then guilt for feeling relieved.

Your body may have been looking for a way out it didn't yet know how to ask for.

Slide 7

These aren't character flaws.

They're patterns that can develop when, at some point, staying quiet felt safer than speaking up. That response made sense then. And with awareness and support, it can shift.

Caption

Sometimes the body knows before the mind does. If you've been noticing these patterns in your relationships, you're not alone in them. Many people who struggled with people-pleasing carry these responses long before they have the words for what's happening. These patterns often develop as ways to stay safe. Sample 2: Carousel

7 slides  ·  Self-trust + Attachment healing

Slide 1 / Cover

What trusting yourself again actually looks like (it's quieter than you might expect)

Slide 2

It looks like noticing the tightness in your chest when something feels off, and actually taking it seriously.

Not overriding it. Not explaining it away. Just noticing.

Slide 3

It looks like saying "I need a minute" without immediately feeling the need to justify it.

Slide 4

It looks like choosing not to over-explain a decision you've already made.

The boundary doesn't need a defense. You don't have to convince anyone.

Slide 5

It looks like letting someone be disappointed with you, and still feeling okay in your own body.

Your worth isn't located in their reaction.

Slide 6

It looks like choosing the steadier, calmer relationship over the one that felt like love because the intensity was familiar.

And trusting that calm can be enough. That steady can be safe.

Slide 7

It doesn't arrive all at once.

Self-trust often builds in small moments, the pause before you say yes, the time you asked for what you needed, the day you put yourself first without apologizing.

Those moments matter, even when they feel small.

Caption

One of the most common things I hear is: "I finally found something calm and steady, so why does it feel so strange?" For many people, a regulated and consistent relationship can feel unfamiliar at first. Not wrong, just new. If you've spent time in relationships where intensity felt like love, it makes sense that calm might take some getting used to. Your nervous system is adjusting to something it hasn't felt before. Rebuilding self-trust often shows up in the quiet moments. The pause before you answer. The day you didn't over-explain. The time you let yourself need something. 05  /  Tai sao em khac

Khong phai ai cung doc truoc khi pitch.

  • 01 Em doc content cua chi truoc khi viet bat cu dieu gi. Hai bai sample tren duoc viet sau khi em doc 20+ bai day du, khong chi scan qua. Do la cach em biet chi dung "often" va "can" de giu do chinh xac lam sang, ma em khong bao gio dung "will" khi chi chua dung.
  • 02 Em hieu su khac biet giua giong noi va topic. Nhieu SMM co the phan tich nhu cau cua audience chi, nhung em tap trung vao nhip cau, tu quen, va cach chi mo bai, vi do la thu ma follower cua chi nhan ra va tin tuong, ngay ca khi ho chua biet tai sao.
  • 03 Em hieu chi ang xay dung mot private practice o dau tu, khong phai mot influencer brand. Content phai phuc vu viec xay dung su tin tuong va uy tin, khong chi de ra so.
  • 04 Khong co template. Moi bai em viet cho chi se khac voi bai em viet cho bat ky ai khac. Vi brand cua chi la cua chi, khong phai cua em.
