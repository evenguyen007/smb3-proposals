why you can't just "start saying no"
(it's not willpower)

Slide 02 · Scene

"i said yes again. i don't even know why."

a client last week. exhausted, didn't want to go. said yes anyway. she wasn't a pushover — something else was happening.

Slide 03 · The real reason

people-pleasing is a nervous system response.

at some point, saying yes kept you safe. your brain learned: yes = safe. no = danger. it's protecting you. 🤍

Slide 04 · What it feels like

you say yes and immediately feel resentful.

you rehearse the no a hundred times. you apologize for things that aren't your fault. you feel relief when plans cancel.

Slide 05 · What helps

you don't "start saying no." you understand what the yes has been protecting.

once my client saw she was protecting an old version of herself — everything shifted. 🤍

Slide 06 · Close

you are not broken. you are not stuck.

you just haven't had someone help you understand the why yet. dallas + telehealth — DMs open. 🤍
