01

Four things I noticed
reading your last 100 posts.

The formula

You have a viral structure and you deploy it instinctively. Both 229K posts use it.

Controversial claim. Preemptive defense. Compassionate reframe. "Regulated does not mean calm 24/7" (228K views). "Talking about trauma too early is harmful" (229K views). "Therapy is just science" (119K). Every one of them leads with something that sounds wrong, catches the objection before the comment section can, then lands on something that makes the viewer feel understood. Your audience does not just watch it. They share it, because it changes how they feel about themselves. Most accounts never develop one format that fires reliably. You have one and have not written it down.

The translation gap

Your nervous system content outperforms your clinical content every time it simplifies.

"Whatever you silence will only get louder" (29K views). "Therapy is just biology" (119K). "Regulated does not mean calm" (228K). The pattern across all of them: you take something clinical and translate it into something a person can physically feel. The posts that underperform are the ones that stay inside the clinical frame. The ones that break through are the ones that close the distance between the lab and the living room. That translation skill is the thing. It is not evenly distributed across your content yet.

The saves gap

You have four comment funnels giving away free resources and nothing for someone to save and come back to.

95 of your last 100 posts are Reels. Your five carousels average 116 likes each with zero video views. You have built a sophisticated lead funnel (weird, panic, rude, consult) that shows you understand conversion — but the content layer that earns saves is missing entirely. Saves are a different signal to the algorithm than views or likes. They indicate depth of engagement and bring followers back. A carousel library built around your nervous system translation voice would turn scroll-past browsers into people who save your content, come back to it, and eventually book a consult.

The bilingual gap

You are the only bilingual clinical psychologist in Kuwait with this reach. You post almost entirely in English.

The Arabic mental health conversation on Instagram is sparse and clinical. Nobody with your reach is posting warm, dark-humored, credentialed Arabic content in the GCC. You have the voice, the credentials, and the cultural context. One Arabic post per week would not dilute your English audience. It would open a discovery lane that no one else is standing in and position you as the psychologist the Arabic-speaking Gulf audience did not know they were waiting for.

Sample A — Carousel

"5 'Anxiety' Symptoms That Are Actually Your Nervous System Doing Its Job"

6-slide educational carousel · Nervous system translation format · CTA: comment "weird"

01 / Cover

You do not have an anxiety problem.

Your nervous system is working exactly the way it was designed to. Here is what that actually looks like.

02

Hypervigilance

You cannot stop scanning the room. Checking the tone in a text. Waiting for something to go wrong.

Your nervous system learned that threats were real. It is not broken. It is doing its job — for an environment that no longer exists.

03

Avoidance

You cancel plans. Go quiet. Withdraw before you get a chance to be hurt.

Avoidance is a protection response, not a character flaw. The nervous system learned: distance equals safety. It is still running that rule.

04

People-pleasing

You say yes when you mean no. You read every room before you speak.

Your nervous system learned that keeping the peace was how you stayed safe. That reflex does not care that you are an adult now. It runs automatically.

05

Overthinking

Your brain will not stop. You replay conversations, run scenarios, plan for outcomes that have not happened.

The brain churns when the body does not feel safe enough to rest. Overthinking is not a thinking problem. It is a nervous system problem.

06 / CTA

This is what regulated means.

Not calm. Not fearless. Safe enough to stay.

If any of these landed, comment "weird" and I will send you 36 free ways to help your nervous system feel safer. No breathing exercises required.
